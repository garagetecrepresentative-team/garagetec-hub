📞 Inbound Call Script SOP-01
⚠️
Never quote a fee before confirming ZIP code. Never offer free estimates to commercial or out-of-area customers.

📞 10-Step Inbound Call Flow

  1. 1
    Greeting
    Say:
    "Thank you for calling Garage Tec Automatic Gates and Garage Door Repair, this is ___. How may I assist you today?"
  2. 2
    Confirm ZIP Code
    Say:
    "What is your ZIP code so I can confirm we service your area?"
    Skip if already provided. Never quote a fee before this step.
  3. 3
    Garage or Gate?
    Ask:
    "Is this for a garage door or a gate?"
    Ask this before asking if we've serviced them before — it changes how you look them up and who gets dispatched.
  4. 4
    New, Returning, or Callback?
    Ask:
    "Have we serviced you before?"
    If new → proceed normally. If returning or a callback → follow the Returning Customer / Callback Procedure card below before continuing.
  5. 5
    Property Type
    Ask:
    "Is this for a residential or commercial property?"
  6. 6
    Commercial Disclaimer (commercial only)
    Say:
    "We do not work with Net 30 payment terms for commercial properties. W-9/COI available on request only — no additional vendor onboarding documents at this time."
  7. 7
    Offer Appointment
    Say:
    "I have a 4:00–7:00 PM appointment available today. Would that work for your schedule?"
  8. 8
    Collect Customer Info
    ✦ Full name (confirm spelling) ✦ Best phone number ✦ Permission to text ✦ Full service address ✦ Description of the issue
  9. 9
    Confirm Appointment
    Say:
    "You are scheduled at [ADDRESS] today between 4:00–7:00 PM. There is a [fee] assessment fee, waived if repairs are completed. You'll get a call/text 30 min before arrival."
  10. 10
    Closing
    Say:
    "If you need to reschedule or have questions, feel free to call us anytime. We look forward to servicing you soon! Have a great day!"

🔁 Returning Customer / Callback Procedure

Notes to Add
  • ✦ When we last serviced them and what was done — pull this from the prior job.
  • ✦ The last service date and attach the old invoice and closing to the new job — required for callback jobs.
Who Gets Dispatched
  1. 1
    Returning customer (non-callback): Send the same technician if available. If not available, the job can be passed to another tech.
  2. 2
    Callback: The installer goes. If the installer isn't available, the seller goes. If the seller isn't available, resort to Daniel or Rolando.
🛑
ALWAYS VERIFY WARRANTY WITH THE CUSTOMER, AND DUPLICATE THE JOB. Confirm warranty status directly with the customer every time, and duplicate the original job in Workiz rather than starting from scratch.

Only offer a free estimate if the customer pushes back on the service fee. Do not lead with it — it is a last-resort save only. New customers only.

Commercial Jobs
🏢
Commercial customers can be accepted if they agree to pay the service fee upon the tech's visit.
  1. 1
    Book the job — confirm they will pay the service fee on the day of the visit
  2. 2
    Tech visits, assesses, and sends the estimate
  3. 3
    Customer approves the estimate → send additional paperwork before any work begins

📲 Outbound Script SOP-02
⚡
Speed is everything. The first company to reach a lead wins the job. Call the moment a lead arrives. Always call first, text second.

📲 7-Step Outbound Call Flow

  1. 1
    Opening
    Say:
    "Hi, may I speak with [Customer Name]?… This is ___ calling from Garage Tec. We'd like to offer you a hassle-free assessment with one of our technicians."
  2. 2
    No Answer?
    Send the approved follow-up text template immediately. Keep attempting contact — do not wait.
  3. 3
    Property Type
    Ask:
    "I just want to confirm — is this for a residential or commercial property?"
    Notate business name if commercial.
  4. 4
    Offer Appointment
    Say:
    "We currently have availability — I can get a technician out to you within the next hour if that works for your schedule?"
    Offer the next available slot within the next hour only if a tech is actually available.
  5. 5
    Verify Contact Details
    ✦ Best phone number ✦ Permission to text ✦ Full service address (required for Thumbtack) ✦ Issue details for the tech
  6. 6
    Confirm Appointment
    Say:
    "Perfect, you are scheduled for a hassle-free assessment at [ADDRESS]. You'll get a call/text 30 min before arrival."
  7. 7
    Closing
    Say:
    "If you need to reschedule or have questions, feel free to give us a call anytime. Thank you for choosing Garage Tec. Have a great day!"

🏠 Onsite Presence Policy Effective Immediately
🛑
A responsible adult (18+) must be present on-site for every appointment. This is a liability requirement and is non-negotiable. Applies to every job type — residential garage door, residential gate, and commercial.

✅ Requirements

  • ✦ The customer, or a responsible adult representative 18+, must be present for the full duration of the technician's visit.
  • ✦ Applies to ALL job types — no exceptions by job category.
  • ✦ Technicians will NOT proceed with any work if no responsible adult is present upon arrival.

📞 Dispatcher Responsibility at Booking

  1. 1
    Always verify at booking that someone 18+ will be present on-site.
  2. 2
    Add a note in Workiz confirming onsite presence was verified.
  3. 3
    Never book or dispatch a job knowing no one will be on-site without first resolving the situation.

⚠️ If the Customer Cannot Be On-Site

  1. 1
    Inform the customer that per company policy, an adult (18+) must be present for liability reasons.
  2. 2
    Offer to reschedule to a time when someone can be present.
  3. 3
    If the customer insists on proceeding without being on-site, documentation must be sent and completed BEFORE dispatch — check with management for the appropriate form.
🛑
Do not dispatch the technician without either (a) a confirmed adult on-site, or (b) completed documentation on file.

🧩 Two Dispatchers on Shift? Split the Work

Applies from 12pm on — a Lead Intake Rep covers calls and lead follow-ups solo from 5am–12pm, so this split kicks in once a second dispatcher comes on.
📞
Dispatcher A — Handles calls and follow-ups on leads.
🔒
Dispatcher B — Handles closings and speaking with technicians.
🤝
No manager on shift? Dispatchers decide between themselves how the split works. We're adults working an office — figure it out together, no need to wait on a manager to assign it.
📢
EVERYONE MUST COMMUNICATE WHAT THEY'RE WORKING ON — whether it's closing a job, adding a job to the schedule, or anything else. Say it out loud or post it in the group so nobody duplicates work or misses something the other person assumed was already handled.

🔒 Job Closing Guide SOP-05
🛑
GATE JOBS — NO EXCEPTIONS: When closing any gate job, remove Mani's name from the Team/assigned techs on that job — even if it's a cancellation. This applies every single time, no matter what. Leaving his name on a job he didn't actually work makes his technician reports inaccurate. This has happened repeatedly — double-check the Team section before you finish closing.

🔒 Job Closing Process

  1. 1
    Add Tech Closing Notes — Paste the tech's notes into the Details tab. Add the date of closing/update at the top. Star the job in Messages while actively working.
  2. 2
    Save ALL Attachments
    ✦ Email correspondence ✦ Receipts ✦ Tax exempts ✦ Parts documentation ✦ Payment confirmations ✦ Tech photos → Attachments tab
  3. 3
    Verify Invoice (Items Tab)
    ✦ Labor separated from parts ✦ Parts taxed correctly ✦ Correct warranty tag added if applicable ✦ All parts costs in the Cost column ✦ Job type is set correctly
  4. 4
    Input Parts Carefully — Enter all parts used, and watch for quantities — double-check part numbers so what's entered actually matches what was used.
  5. 5
    Verify Payment — Confirm payment is correct and fully recorded in the Payments tab.
  6. 6
    Invoice Review & Sending
    ✍️ Check for the customer's signature 📄 Invoice NOT sent → send and request signature ✅ Invoice signed but NOT sent → send without requesting signature again
  7. 7
    Send the Closing — Promptly — Send closings in a timely manner, especially to external companies. See routing table and the tax note below.
  8. 8
    Gate Job? Remove Mani from the Team (NO EXCEPTIONS) — Before finishing, check the Team section on every gate job and remove Mani's name if he's listed — even on a cancellation. Do this every time, without exception.
  9. 9
    Final Step — Change job status to "Done".
💲
EXTERNAL COMPANY CLOSINGS — NO TAXES. When sending a closing to an external company, leave the tax amount out of what you send. Send closings promptly — don't let them sit.
📊
Why job type matters: Setting the correct job type on every closing is how we track which job types are closing most often — get it right so the reporting is accurate.

🔁 Universal Lead Follow-Up Procedure — Every Source, Plus Existing Customers

🎯
We are usually the first contact a potential client has with any company. How we follow up is what determines whether we save that potential client — or lose them to whoever calls back first.
📣
Applies to LSA, Thumbtack, Angi, Yelp, Facebook, PPC, Networx, and ZZZ. If a lead does not wish to book, doesn't want a free estimate or phone estimate, and every avenue has been exhausted — or they simply ask for a callback — follow this procedure. A lead that doesn't book today is still a potential customer.
📞
Same applies to our existing customers. If you're calling someone who's already our customer — for anything, not just a new lead — and they don't answer, work the exact same follow-up protocol below. Don't treat existing customers any differently just because we've worked with them before.
When the Lead Doesn't Book:
  1. 1
    Add them into Workiz if they aren't already in the system.
  2. 2
    Set status to Pending and move to the Unscheduled tab.
  3. 3
    Tag the necessary tags for the lead source (see How to Input External Company / Job Source above) and set the correct job type.
  4. 4
    Schedule the follow-up dates using the timeline below — don't just note "follow up later," put actual dates on it.
  5. 5
    Add notes documenting every contact attempt and customer response.

📅 Follow-Up Timeline (No Answer)

💡
Same timeline for every source and for existing customers who don't answer. During any downtime, check the Unscheduled tab and actively work through open follow-ups.
  1. 1
    Initial call — Call the lead directly to try to schedule an appointment
  2. 2
    No answer → text immediately — Send a text message right away
  3. 3
    +5 hours — Send a second text message
  4. 4
    Next day — Follow up again
  5. 5
    +3 days — Final follow-up attempt
🛑
Close the lead only after Step 5 with no response — or sooner if the customer declines. Add detailed notes for every step.
